# Lateral Thinking Course (Lateral Thinking Mind, 2014) — full read

Coverage: 100 pages (preface, activities 1–10, two stages of thinking, imagination, random inputs, challenging concepts, association of ideas, REST, FAQs, problem definition).

## Transferred
- Two stages: **idea (creative) stage** without judgement, then **processing (logical) stage**; logic is abundant, idea generation is the bottleneck.
- **REST — Random External Stimulus Technique**: create "happy accidents" on demand with random words.
- **Association laws** (Aristotle, as presented): contiguity, similarity, contrast.
- **Challenge established concepts**; ask "why" repeatedly.
- **Define the problem**: state it clearly, simplify into components, consider the obvious solution, detach emotionally, use imagination.
- Limits: brain/neuron statistics and "infinite subconscious" claims are motivational rhetoric, not evidence; ideas are hypotheses.
