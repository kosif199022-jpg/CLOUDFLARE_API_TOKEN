# The Conflict Thesaurus, Volume 2 (Ackerman & Puglisi) — full read

Coverage: 715 pages; craft chapters + ~150 conflict entries in 6 categories (Loss of Control, Power Struggles, Losing an Advantage, Ego-Related Conflicts, Dangers and Threats, Miscellaneous Challenges), GMC+S tracker, climax troubleshooting; vol. 1 categories listed at the end (Relationship Friction, Failures and Mistakes, …).

## Transferred rules (own words)
- Central conflict is one of six: vs character, society, nature, technology, supernatural, self.
- Conflict operates on levels: central → story (macro, looming) → scene (micro) → inner; subplots must feed the central conflict.
- Conflict is the "golden thread" linking plot and arc: inciting incident, all-is-lost, dark night, climax (outer clash won/lost by the inner resolution).
- Amp-up levers: uncertain outcome; withhold what the character needs; personal stakes; no-win choices; keep the character moving; shake up the team; tighten the clock.
- Agency: the protagonist must drive events through choices, not merely react.
- Pitfalls: low/no stakes, contrived conflict, too much conflict (episodic, exhausting), navel-gazing (target ≈80% action / 20% introspection), resolving relationship conflict too fast.
- Entry anatomy used for generation: examples · minor complications · potentially disastrous results · resulting emotions · internal struggles · negative traits that worsen it · impact on basic needs (physiological, safety, belonging, esteem, self-actualisation) · positive outcomes.
