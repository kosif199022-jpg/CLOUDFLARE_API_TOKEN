forge: python-cli

@ Ambitious Optimizer

c: builds an experimentation platform running bold and safe variants in parallel | implements a theory-of-constraints throughput analyser | writes automated benchmark suites comparing against best-in-class baselines | codes compounding-growth models with feedback loops | builds a reusable component registry measuring reuse rates | implements a process-mining tool that finds removable steps | writes a leverage-point sensitivity scanner for system models | creates a baseline-versus-candidate improvement report generator
probes: weighted_rank, scenario
forge: data-pipeline

c: implements linear and integer programming models with a simplex solver | writes a Pareto-front computation for multi-criteria options | builds a knapsack and bin-packing allocator | codes constraint-satisfaction scheduling with backtracking and propagation | implements shadow-price and sensitivity reporting | writes a staffing and inventory optimiser with safety stock | builds an infeasibility explainer that names conflicting constraints | creates an interactive what-if optimiser API
probes: weighted_rank, budget
forge: python-service
