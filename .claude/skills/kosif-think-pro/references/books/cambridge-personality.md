# The Cambridge Handbook of Personality Psychology (Corr & Matthews, 2009) — full read

Coverage: 906 pages, 46 chapters in 8 parts (foundations, description/measurement, development/health, biological, cognitive, social/cultural, psychopathology, applied).

## Transferred (own words, with chapter anchors)
- **Five-Factor Model with 30 facets** (ch.9, NEO-PI-R): N1 anxiety, N2 angry hostility, N3 depression, N4 self-consciousness, N5 impulsiveness, N6 vulnerability; E1 warmth, E2 gregariousness, E3 assertiveness, E4 activity, E5 excitement seeking, E6 positive emotions; O1 fantasy, O2 aesthetics, O3 feelings, O4 actions, O5 ideas, O6 values; A1 trust, A2 straightforwardness, A3 altruism, A4 compliance, A5 modesty, A6 tender-mindedness; C1 competence, C2 order, C3 dutifulness, C4 achievement striving, C5 self-discipline, C6 deliberation.
- **Traits × situations** (ch.2–3, ch.27 CAPS): people show stable *if … then …* behavioural signatures — behaviour depends on the situation class, and two people with the same average can differ sharply by situation.
- **Self-regulation** (ch.24): goal-directed feedback loops — compare current state with a reference value, act to reduce the discrepancy, monitor rate of progress.
- **Self-determination theory** (ch.25): universal needs for autonomy, competence and relatedness drive motivation quality.
- **Applied/work** (ch.43–44): personality predicts performance and safety behaviour modestly and context-dependently.

## Where it lives
- Personality Council: each of the 14 reasoning profiles is now a facet vector + if-then activation rules (context-conditioned), never a diagnosis of users.
- Character creation (image/video/story): Character Lock gains a facet profile and if-then signatures so acting, expression and choices stay consistent across shots (`references/kosif-image-studio/character-psychology.md`).
- Interaction style: offer choices (autonomy), explain reasons and show progress (competence), warm respectful tone (relatedness).
- Hard limit: never infer or label a real user's personality or disorder.
