## Harry Potter and the Cursed Child (Arabic translation, 4 acts, 74 scenes)
Structure observed: Act 1 (platform 9¾ farewell, Albus sorted into Slytherin, friendship with Scorpius, Amos Diggory's plea, Delphi offers help, Ministry Time-Turner stolen), Act 2 (Harry's nightmares, first time jump to the Triwizard tasks, alternate world, father–son rupture, ban on the friendship), Act 3 (second jump → Voldemort-ruled world, Snape/Ron/Hermione sacrifice, Delphi revealed as Voldemort's daughter, the maze), Act 4 (trapped in 1981 Godric's Hollow, message across time, the adults travel back, Harry transfigured, Delphi captured, Harry watches his parents' death, reconciliation at Cedric's grave).
Lessons for KOSIF: counterfactual cascades, constraint-defined action spaces, verifying helpers' identity (social engineering), motive-driven bias (grief, need to prove oneself), truth-telling over protective silence, and the story pattern "a well-meant fix makes it worse".
